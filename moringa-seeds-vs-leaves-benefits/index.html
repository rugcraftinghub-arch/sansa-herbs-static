Brewing Moringa Tea (Best Method)

Moringa tea is one of the simplest ways to use the leaves.

Use water heated just below boiling, around 185°F.

Steep 1 teaspoon of dried leaves for 5 to 7 minutes.

This helps preserve delicate nutrients while giving you a mild, earthy tea.

You can also explore other herbal teas like shining bush tea, which is commonly used in Caribbean backyards for cooling and blood pressure support.
