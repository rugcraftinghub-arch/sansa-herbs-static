About Sansa Herbs

Welcome! I'm Stacie Leigh, the creator of Sansa Herbs.

My journey with tropical botanicals began as a way to connect with the lush rainforest environment surrounding my home. What started as an interest in the local flora quickly evolved into a dedicated practice of studying, growing, and crafting with plants.

When I transitioned back to city living, I was determined to bring that botanical connection with me. I began cultivating my own collection of tropical herbs in containers, experimenting with both native and non-native species. What began as a few pots has evolved into a thriving, productive garden, proving that you don’t need vast acreage to cultivate a meaningful botanical space.

As an expert apothecary and content creator, I am dedicated to the art of botanical studies. My garden is my laboratory, where I explore propagation techniques, harvest methods, and the creative ways to incorporate these plants into home-crafted teas, skincare, and botanical rituals.

Through Sansa Herbs, I share my methods for growing tropical botanicals in containers, making the process accessible for any home environment. Whether you have a sunny windowsill or a small patio, I am here to help you design and maintain your own thriving botanical toolkit.
