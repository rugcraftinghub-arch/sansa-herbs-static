Top 10 aromatic herb Garden Tools

1. Seed Trays or Small Pots

Perfect for starting seeds in a controlled environment. Keeps seedlings organized and healthy.

2. Rich, Well-Draining Potting Soil

Supports healthy root development and consistent moisture retention.

This is especially important for herbs like marshmallow, which prefer consistently moist, well-draining soil →

3. LED Grow Lights

Provides optimal light spectrum for indoor seedlings and faster germination.

4. Hori Hori Garden Knife (All-in-One Precision Tool)

The Hori Hori knife is a versatile tool used for transplanting, digging, cutting roots, and harvesting herbs. Its sharp edge and depth markings make it especially useful for precise planting and working in small spaces.

5. Basic Hand Tool Set (Trowel, Pruners, Weeder)

A simple tool set covers everyday gardening tasks like digging, loosening soil, and handling plants. Ideal for beginners starting their herbal garden.

6. Watering Can or Spray Bottle

Gently waters seedlings without disturbing roots.

7. Plant Labels

Keeps track of multiple herbs in trays or pots.

8. Moisture / 3-in-1 Soil Meter

3-in-1 soil meter measures moisture, pH, and light to keep your seedlings healthy and thriving.

9. Herb Drying Rack or Hanging Bundle Kit

Used after harvest to dry herbs for teas, oils, and storage. Adds a “from seed to use” lifecycle element.

10. Kneeler / Mat (Optional)

Adds comfort when working at ground level and protects knees.

Putting It All Together: Step-by-Step

  1. Unbox your Herb Garden Kit and prepare seed trays.
  2. Fill trays with potting soil and sow seeds according to instructions. If you’re growing herbs like marshmallow, follow this step-by-step guide
  3. Cover with humidity dome to maintain moisture.
  4. Place under LED grow lights; adjust as seedlings grow.
  5. Monitor soil moisture and pH with tools from your kit.
  6. Transplant seedlings with Hori Hori knife or trowel.
  7. Water gently using your watering can or spray bottle.
  8. Keep tools organized in the tote for easy access.
  9. Use heat mat for extra germination support in cooler months.
  10. Track growth with plant labels and adjust care as needed.

Herb Gardening Tips

  • Rotate trays under lights for even growth.
  • Keep a watering schedule to avoid over- or under-watering.
  • Start with easy herbs like calendula or marshmallow for success.
  • Always sanitize hand tools to prevent imbalance.
