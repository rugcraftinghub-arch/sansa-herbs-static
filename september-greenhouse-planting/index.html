As the first crisp mornings signal autumn's approach, smart gardeners know this is prime time for September greenhouse planting. Most people think the growing season is winding down in September. With a greenhouse, it's actually when the real planning starts.

JUMP TO THE FALL PLANTING CHECKLIST

September is actually the best time to plant, not just clean up what's left of summer. This is when you set up a steady supply of fresh vegetables for the months ahead. The crops you plant now will keep your greenhouse producing through winter and give you a strong head start for spring. With the right setup, you’ll shield tender plants from cold snaps, enjoy fresh greens all season, and even grow herbs indoors with the help of grow lights as daylight fades.

Your September Greenhouse Planting Benefits

Here's why September is worth paying attention to if you've got a greenhouse (or you're thinking about getting one).

  • ☑️ Immediate Protection: As temperatures begin to fluctuate, your greenhouse becomes a sanctuary for frost-sensitive plants and delicate seedlings. No more anxiously checking weather forecasts or scrambling to cover plants when an unexpected cold snap hits.
  • ☑️ Extended Growing Season: Cool-weather crops like kale, Brussels sprouts, and winter lettuce thrive in the controlled environment of a greenhouse. You'll be harvesting fresh vegetables when others are relying on store-bought produce.
  • ☑️ Year-Round Productivity: You can start seeds in late winter, move seedlings out in spring, and keep herbs and vegetables going all year with one setup. Start seeds in late winter, nurture seedlings in spring, and maintain herbs and vegetables throughout the year.
  • ☑️ Pest Protection: The controlled environment keeps many common garden pests at bay, reducing the need for pesticides and ensuring healthier plants.
