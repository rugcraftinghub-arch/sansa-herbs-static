I'm Growing These Kit Herbs in Trinidad — Here's What I Found

Most aromatic herb seed kits are marketed for temperate climates like the US, UK, and Canada. But I've been growing several of these herbs right here in Trinidad — and the results have been impressive enough that I confidently recommend this kit to gardeners in any climate.

Here's what's actually growing in my garden right now:

Marshmallow (Althaea officinalis)

I started my marshmallow plant from seed in a pot and have watched it grow from a small seedling into a healthy, established plant. It's not flowering yet but the growth has been steady and consistent even in Trinidad's tropical heat.

I've used marshmallow leaves for smoking blends and made marshmallow root tea — one of the most soothing herbal teas for throat and gut inflammation I've tried. The root has a naturally sweet, mucilaginous quality that coats and calms.

Calendula (Calendula officinalis)

My calendula seedlings are currently in trays and almost ready for transplanting. Even at the seedling stage they look healthy and vigorous. Calendula is one of the most versatile herbs in the kit — used for skin soothing, wound care, and inflammation.

Yarrow (Achillea millefolium)

Yarrow has been one of the easier herbs to establish in my garden. Known for its wound-soothing and anti-inflammatory properties, it's a must-have in any home apothecary.

Chamomile (Matricaria chamomilla)

Chamomile is perhaps the most well known herb in the kit and for good reason. Mine has been growing reliably and is one of the first herbs I'd recommend new gardeners start with. The flowers make a gentle calming tea that's hard to beat.
