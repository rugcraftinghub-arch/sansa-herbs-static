Basic DIY Turmeric Scrub Recipe

You only need a few ingredients to make a simple turmeric scrub that works for both face and body:

  • 1 tablespoon turmeric powder
  • 2 tablespoons sugar (use brown sugar for face, raw sugar for body)
  • 1 to 2 tablespoons carrier oil (such as jojoba, sweet almond, or coconut)
  • Optional: 1 teaspoon honey or aloe vera gel

Instructions:

  1. Mix all ingredients in a small bowl.
  2. Apply to clean, damp skin in gentle circular motions.
  3. Leave on for 3–5 minutes.
  4. Rinse thoroughly and pat dry.

Tip: For facial use, stick to brown sugar and jojoba oil. For body scrubs, raw sugar and coconut oil add extra grit and moisture.

Safety Note
While turmeric is traditionally used for skin brightening, please consult a dermatologist if you have sensitive skin or active skin conditions. Always perform a patch test on your inner arm before applying any new scrub to your face.

Body Scrub Variations

You can customize your turmeric scrub depending on your skin type and the area you're treating. Here are a few body-friendly options:

1. Moisturizing Yogurt Scrub

Combine turmeric, yogurt, and brown sugar for a soothing, creamy mix. This is great for dry or dull skin on the arms or chest.

2. Brightening Oil Scrub

Mix turmeric with a rich carrier oil and coarse sugar for areas like knees, elbows, and feet. It leaves skin glowing and hydrated.

3. Clove and Turmeric Scrub (for Back or Butt)

Add a pinch of finely ground clove to the base scrub for antibacterial support. This works best on areas like the back or butt that are prone to body acne. Avoid using clove on your face and always patch test first.

How to Use the Scrub

Start with clean, damp skin. Scoop a small amount of the scrub and apply it to your face using gentle circular motions. This helps exfoliate dead skin cells and improve circulation.

Once the scrub is evenly applied, let it sit for three to five minutes.
During this time, the yogurt’s lactic acid and turmeric’s anti-inflammatory properties can work on the skin.

Rinse off with warm water and pat dry. Follow with a light moisturizer.
