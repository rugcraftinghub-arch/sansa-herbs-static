What is an Affiliate Relationship?

An affiliate promotes products or services and receives a commission when purchases are made through their unique referral links. These commissions are paid by the companies offering the products/services and do not result in any additional cost to you as the purchaser.

Our Commitment to You

We want to be clear about several important points:
